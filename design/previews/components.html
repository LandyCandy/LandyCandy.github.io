// project 04 · 2026 · R&D · confidential-safe

In-line NIR inspection for a first-of-its-kind coating process

Challenge

A brand-new process with no inspection precedent: the defects of interest were invisible under visible light, and nobody could yet say what "good" looked like.

Approach

Built an NIR imaging rig to make the failure mode visible, then an architecture that let the team relabel, retrain, and redeploy on the line without touching the machine control code.
